About Me

Not a typical
student story.

Hey, I'm Gungun — a 19-year-old CS student specialising in AI/ML, frontend developer, and the founder of Outfevibe. I decided to build something real long before I graduate.

I didn't start with fancy hardware or a team. I started with curiosity, YouTube, and a laptop that crashed more than it compiled. Every bug was a lesson. Every rebuild made the next version stronger.

What drives me isn't just code — it's the intersection of fashion, technology, and human identity. The idea that AI can help someone discover who they are through what they wear — that's the spark behind Outfevibe.

I'm building in public, learning in real-time, and shipping despite fear. I believe the gap between an idea and reality is just consistent, uncomfortable work — and I'm here for every bit of it.

The Story

Not a straight
line to here.

Chapter 01 · The Beginning
🌱 Started from YouTube tutorials
Like most self-taught developers, it started with a browser, YouTube, and a burning question — "can I actually build this?" No bootcamp, no mentor, just curiosity and a slow internet connection that tested patience like nothing else.
Chapter 02 · The Grind
💻 Built on a laptop that kept crashing
Limited hardware didn't mean limited ambition. Crashes became a feature — they forced me to understand what I was building. Every forced reboot meant a mental review of the code. Some of my best debugging happened in the seconds before a restart.
Chapter 03 · The Failures
🔥 Projects that broke, ideas that flopped
Not everything worked. In fact, most things didn't — at first. But each failure mapped the territory. I learned Flask before FastAPI. I learned what users didn't want before understanding what they needed. Failure was the curriculum.
Chapter 04 · The Spark
✨ Fashion + AI = something worth building
The idea for Outfevibe came from a personal frustration — fashion advice online wasn't built for real Indian bodies, real skin tones, or real budgets. If AI could solve personalization in every other industry, why not fashion? That question changed everything.
Chapter 05 · The Launch
🚀 Launched Outfevibe — learning while shipping
Launched while still in college. Built auth, AI flows, outfit cards, SEO, email campaigns, and seller portal — learning a new technology the same week it needed to ship. Secured 3rd place at Internal SIH along the way.
Chapter 06 · Right Now
🌕 Interning. Building. Not done.
Currently interning at Unified Mentor as a Frontend Developer while running Outfevibe and building DSP. 900+ visitors, 2 live projects, and 1 more chapter just beginning. The story isn't over. It's just getting interesting.
